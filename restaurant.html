Our Story

The House of
Flowers

"Busaba" is Thailand's national flower — the Plumeria — and "Ruen" means a place where those of noble spirit come to gather. Ruen Busaba is more than a restaurant. It is an invitation to step into the soul of ancient Thailand, right in the heart of Dhaka.

Every corner of our space tells a story: hand-carved wood and stone panels, bamboo ceilings, Thai mythological motifs, and a wishing bell at the entrance that carries the hopes of every guest who rings it.

Certified by the Royal Thai Government's Thai Select Signature programme — the highest official recognition for authentic Thai cuisine abroad — Ruen Busaba stands as Dhaka's premier destination for the true flavours of Thailand.

What sets us apart

The Ruen Busaba Difference

Thai Select Signature

Certified by the Royal Thai Government — the most prestigious recognition for authentic Thai cuisine outside Thailand. One of very few in South Asia.

Ingredients from Thailand

Every spice, paste, herb and seasoning is sourced directly from Thailand — flown in regularly to ensure our dishes taste exactly as they do in Bangkok.

Thai-Trained Chefs

Our kitchen is led by Iron Master Chef Chakri Nualphen — trained in Thailand's finest establishments — who brings uncompromising authenticity to every plate.
