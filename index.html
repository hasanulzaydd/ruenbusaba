Meet the master behind our culinary artistries

Iron Master Chef

Nualphen

Chef Chakri Nualphen brings decades of authentic Thai culinary mastery to Dhaka. Trained in Thailand's finest kitchens, he leads our brigade with an unwavering commitment to tradition — sourcing every spice, herb, and ingredient directly from Thailand.

His philosophy is simple: cook with soul, serve with pride. Every dish on our menu is a testament to the pure, unadulterated flavours of Thai cuisine — unchanged, uncompromised.
